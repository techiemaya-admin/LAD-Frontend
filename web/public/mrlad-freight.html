Discovery

Six questions that find the leak.

Run these on your own desk before you speak to anyone. If three of them make you uncomfortable, we can help. If none of them do, you do not need us yet - and we will tell you so on the call.

01 How many quotations went out last month - and how many were chased more than twice?

If nobody can answer that from a system, there is no follow-up process, only good intentions. This is the largest single leak in freight sales, and it is entirely mechanical to close.

02 Which channel produced your last five new shippers?

If the honest answer is “referrals, and one person's contacts”, your growth ceiling is that person's calendar - and it leaves when they do.

03 What happens to an enquiry that lands on WhatsApp at 21:00 on a Friday?

Forwarders lose far more on response time than on rate. If the answer is “Monday”, you are losing shipments you had already won.

04 How many shippers who moved cargo with you last year have not been contacted since?

Pull the list before you answer. It is usually in the hundreds. They already trust you - they simply stopped hearing from you.

05 Who is prospecting during peak, while ops is under water?

If the answer is nobody, the feast-and-famine cycle is not bad luck. It is structural, and it will repeat next season on schedule.

06 What does one new shipper cost you to win?

If you cannot say, you cannot compare a marketing spend against a salesperson - so every growth decision you make is a guess.

None of this is a knowledge problem. Every forwarder knows the quote should be chased on day two. The gap is execution - and execution is the part Mr LAD does, every day, straight through the peak.

What Mr LAD is

One system runs the whole motion. Not one channel with a dashboard on top.

Mr LAD builds your ideal-customer profile, finds real people who match it, and works each one as a person - connecting on LinkedIn, following up on WhatsApp, emailing, calling by voice. It handles the reply, the objection, the reschedule and the fifth follow-up nobody ever sends. Every conversation is pushed toward one outcome you define: a meeting booked, an order placed, a quotation sent.

It sells the outcome, not the activity

Every agent optimises for one event you define - a booked meeting, a placed order, a completed quotation. It's measured and attributed, so you see what each one cost.

Many channels, one prospect record

LinkedIn, WhatsApp, email and voice all read and write the same timeline. A WhatsApp reply is visible to the LinkedIn agent - so nobody gets two conversations from you at once.

Safe to point at your real name

Design the play once, it runs daily

Choose a playbook, or build your own: who to target, what to say, what happens when they don't answer, which channel to fall back to. Save it and redeploy it in a click.

Quality of output

Messages people actually reply to.

Before Mr LAD writes a word, it reads the prospect: their company, their role, what they've been posting about. Then it writes like someone who did the homework - because it did.

Researched, then written

Company research, role and seniority, enrichment and recent activity all land in the message before it's drafted. Not a merge field with a first name in it.

Reviewed before it sends

A supervising model reads every outbound message for tone and context. When it isn't sure, it escalates to you rather than guessing - and a separate check blocks broken merge fields outright.

It knows when to stop

Follow-up sequences cancel themselves the moment a prospect replies on any channel. No awkward "just bumping this" after someone already said yes.
